### 🚀 Flagship Project: ScoreSentinel (v2.0)

> **Live Demo:** [transactionmonitoring.vercel.app](https://transactionmonitoring.vercel.app) • **Repository:** [atulkrishnan18-wq/transactionmonitoring](https://github.com/atulkrishnan18-wq/transactionmonitoring)

**ScoreSentinel** is an enterprise-grade AML Transaction Risk Scoring Engine and Model Risk Management (MRM) platform.

#### Key Architectural Highlights:
* ⚙️ **Configurable Enterprise Scenario Engine:** Parameterized detection rules decoupling business logic from code:
  * `SCEN-STRUC-01`: Smurfing & Sub-CTR Structuring (*BSA 31 U.S.C. § 5324 / FinCEN Advisory FIN-2012-A008*)
  * `SCEN-VEL-01`: Rapid Fund Movement & Pass-Through Mule Account (*FATF Mule Networks / FinCEN FIN-2020-A003*)
  * `SCEN-CORR-01`: High-Risk Corridor Spike & Velocity Deviation (*FATF Rec 19 / OFAC Risk Matrix*)
* 🕸️ **Project MuleCatcher™:** Advanced cluster intelligence identifying Fan-In/Fan-Out patterns, Shared Device Nexus, and Dormant-to-Active spikes across coordinated mule networks.
* ⚖️ **Dual-Resolution Audit Lock:** 
  * *Screening Matches:* Strict enforcement of the **3-Point Identifier Standard** to eliminate false-positive rubber-stamping.
  * *Behavioral Risk:* Enforced compliance rationale before alert clearance.
* 📊 **SR 11-7 Model Governance:** Defensible Composite Risk Scoring (CRS) calibrated to eliminate alert fatigue while maintaining high-risk sensitivity.
